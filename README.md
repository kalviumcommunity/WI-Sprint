# Consulting Utilization & Operations Analyzer

## Overview

Consulting firms manage large amounts of operational data such as employee
timesheets, project allocation records, and client billing exports.

However, this information is often scattered across different files and
systems, making it difficult for managers to understand how efficiently
their teams and projects are being utilized.

This project aims to provide a centralized platform for analyzing operational
data and identifying hidden inefficiencies that reduce billable utilization.

---

## Problem Statement

A consulting firm manages employee timesheets, project allocation records,
and client billing exports, but managers cannot identify which operational
inefficiencies quietly reduce billable utilization.

The system focuses on transforming these operational records into meaningful
insights that help managers understand where billable time is being lost and
where improvements can be made.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze employee timesheet records
- Track project allocation and employee utilization
- Identify billable and non-billable working hours
- Analyze client billing information
- Detect under-utilization and operational inefficiencies
- Provide managers with clear and actionable insights
- Improve visibility into overall billable utilization

---

## Key Features

### Employee Utilization

Analyze employee working hours and identify the difference between
billable and non-billable time.

### Project Allocation

Track how employees are allocated across different projects and identify
potential allocation gaps.

### Billing Analysis

Analyze client billing records and compare billed work against recorded
employee activity.

### Efficiency Insights

Highlight patterns that may indicate:

- Low employee utilization
- Excessive non-billable hours
- Under-allocated resources
- Project allocation inefficiencies
- Differences between recorded work and billing data

### Management Dashboard

Provide managers with a centralized view of utilization, project allocation,and another 
billing performance, and operational insights.
